# Social proof

> **Evidence that other people chose this.** Numbers, names and quotes — each a claim the page has
> to stand behind, so cite figures and only publish quotes and logos you have permission to use.

## Which one?

| Component                                                                 | Use it when                                          | Not when                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [StatsSection](?path=/docs/social-proof-statssection--docs)                 | Two to four headline figures back the pitch          | Figures must count up on scroll — render `AnimatedNumber` from a client component       |
| [LogoCloudSection](?path=/docs/social-proof-logocloudsection--docs)         | A quick "trusted by" or "connects to" strip          | Logos orbiting your mark as a hero visual — [OrbitRing](?path=/docs/showcase-orbitring--docs) |
| [TestimonialSection](?path=/docs/social-proof-testimonialsection--docs)     | Quotes from customers in the buyer's position        | A rotating carousel — keep that client-side in the app                                  |

## Shared limitations

- Nothing here animates with JavaScript. The logo marquee is CSS only and holds still under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Decorative marks — trend arrows, stars, headshots, the marquee's second copy — are hidden from
  assistive tech; the text around them carries the meaning.
